PP Life Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Introduction

PP Life ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed by PPLife. We take your privacy very seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app.

Data Collection and Storage

PPLife reads health data on your device and stores app settings and records locally. Wooden Fish records can sync between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch. We also use TelemetryDeck to receive app usage and technical analytics, as described below.

Health Data

With your permission, PPLife reads relevant data from Apple HealthKit to display health trends and estimates. We do not send raw HealthKit records, such as individual heart-rate, HRV or sleep measurements, as analytics events. Some analytics events include the displayed stress category, your chosen step goal, whether health data was available, record counts or count ranges, the selected time-range category, and the data source. These are derived health or fitness information and technical metadata; they help us understand feature use, loading and navigation behavior.

You can change PPLife's access to health data in Apple Health or your device settings.

Subscriptions

PP Life offers auto-renewable subscriptions to unlock premium features. Subscription payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not have access to your payment information.

Third-Party Services

We use TelemetryDeck to understand which features are used and to improve the app. Events include screen visits, character choices, Wooden Fish usage and milestones, settings changes, membership status, purchase-flow results, and diagnostic categories such as network or purchase-verification failures. Purchase events describe the plan, entry point and outcome; they do not include payment-card details or StoreKit transaction identifiers.

The SDK also supplies technical information such as app version, device model, operating-system version, language, session information, and a processed device-based identifier used to distinguish app installations. TelemetryDeck applies its identifier-processing and privacy protections as described in its Privacy FAQ. We do not use these events for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites.

Children's Privacy

PP Life is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
